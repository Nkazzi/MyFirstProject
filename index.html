The Sol Plaatje University, which had provisionally been referred to as the University of the Northern Cape,
opened in Kimberley, South Africa, in 2014, accommodating a modest initial intake of 135 students.
The student complement is expected to increase gradually towards a target of 7,500 students by 2024.
